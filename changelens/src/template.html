How the AI works, and what it cannot decide

A five-class TF-IDF + logistic-regression model suggests a service category and shows influential words. It was trained on 80 synthetic English examples for the earlier ScopeProof prototype. A separate lexical search uses TF-IDF cosine similarity and normalized word overlap to retrieve related original clauses. Quantity, exclusion and deadline checks are explicit rules, not learned reasoning. The model scores are uncalibrated.

ChangeLens does not interpret contracts, estimate a fair price, verify signatures, send messages or move money. It can miss paraphrases, implied scope, mixed-language text, dates and complex quantities. Every item requires a person to choose a decision and explain it. A SHA-256 digest only detects changes to an exported record; it does not prove identity, agreement or tamper-proof storage.

Briefs are held in browser memory and are cleared on refresh. No model provider receives them. Downloaded records contain your text; share them deliberately. The hosting platform may maintain ordinary access logs. All examples are synthetic. No customer, revenue or time-saved claim has been measured.
